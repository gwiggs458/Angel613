The beginning

It Began at 6:13

For several weeks, I found myself waking at the same time: 6:13 in the morning.

At first, it was simply something I noticed. But as it continued, curiosity led me to explore the meaning associated with those three numbers, including interpretations of angel numbers and the 613 commandments in Jewish tradition.

That curiosity inspired me to create 613COIN. Over time, the journey grew into something more personal: Angel 613, a community built around kindness, gratitude, encouragement, and the wisdom we can share with one another.

Eventually, those reflections became my first book, 613 — A Ray of Light. What began with a number became a reminder to live with purpose and bring a little more light into the world.

The meaning of this journey is found not only in how it began, but in the good we choose to do along the way.

Our purpose

A Little Light Can Go a Long Way

We don't need perfect days to practice gratitude, and we don't need grand gestures to show kindness. Sometimes a thoughtful word or a quiet act of care is enough to make a difference.

30 Days of Light

Thirty opportunities to pause, reflect, and put kindness into action. Open any day to explore a short companion reflection and a simple action you can take today.

DAY 01A New Beginning+

Every morning gives us another opportunity to begin. Yesterday's problems may still be here, and we may not have all the answers.

Today’s Action
Take one small step toward something that matters to you.

DAY 02The Power of a Kind Word+

A sincere compliment, a thoughtful thank-you, or a little encouragement takes only a moment. Yet those words can help someone feel seen and appreciated.

Today’s Action
Offer someone a sincere word of encouragement.

DAY 03Appreciate What Is Here+

It's easy to focus on the next goal, opportunity, or accomplishment. But while we're looking ahead, we may miss what is already good: a familiar voice, a peaceful moment, or someone who cares about us.

Today’s Action
Write down three things you appreciate today.

DAY 04Give Yourself Some Grace+

Some days don't go as planned. We make mistakes, feel tired, or fall short of what we hoped to accomplish.

Today’s Action
Speak to yourself with the patience you would offer a friend.

DAY 05Someone Needs Encouragement+

Not everyone who needs encouragement will ask for it. Someone may seem confident while quietly struggling, or carry a difficult responsibility without saying a word.

Today’s Action
Reach out to someone who may need encouragement.

DAY 06Small Progress Matters+

Progress isn't always a major achievement. Sometimes it's learning one thing, making a better decision, finishing a difficult task, or trying again after a setback.

Today’s Action
Recognize one small piece of progress you've made.

DAY 07A Moment of Peace+

There is always another task, message, or responsibility waiting for our attention. It's easy to believe every moment must be productive.

Today’s Action
Spend five quiet minutes without distractions.

DAY 08Choose Understanding+

It's easy to form an opinion when we know only part of someone's story. We see an action or hear a few words, but we may not know the worries and experiences behind them.

Today’s Action
Listen to someone before offering your opinion.

DAY 09Your Efforts Have Value+

Not every worthwhile effort is noticed. We fulfill responsibilities, help others, and keep going—sometimes without appreciation or even a thank-you.

Today’s Action
Acknowledge an effort you usually overlook.

DAY 10Don't Compare Your Journey+

We begin from different places, face different challenges, and discover opportunities at different times. Comparing our lives to someone else's can make us overlook how far we've come.

Today’s Action
Focus on your next step instead of someone else's pace.

DAY 11The Gift of Listening+

Sometimes the kindest thing we can offer is our full attention. We may feel tempted to give advice, find an immediate solution, or share a similar experience of our own.

Today’s Action
Give someone your full attention today.

DAY 12Let Go of What You Cannot Control+

Some situations call for action. Others call for patience.

Today’s Action
Write down one worry you can release for now.

DAY 13Be Someone's Reason to Smile+

A friendly greeting, a lighthearted conversation, or a thoughtful gesture can brighten an ordinary moment. We may never know how much someone needed that small bit of warmth.

Today’s Action
Do something simple to make another person smile.

DAY 14Keep Learning+

Experience teaches us a great deal, but there is always something more to discover: a new skill, a different perspective, or a better way to approach an old problem.

Today’s Action
Learn one new thing and share it thoughtfully.

DAY 15Halfway Through, Still Moving Forward+

When we focus only on what's unfinished, it's easy to overlook what we've already accomplished. We see the distance ahead but forget the effort, lessons, and difficult moments that brought us this far.

Today’s Action
Notice how far you've come, not just how far remains.

DAY 16Kindness Without Recognition+

Some acts of kindness happen quietly. Someone offers help, shares useful information, or makes another person's day easier without telling anyone about it.

Today’s Action
Do a kind deed without expecting recognition.

DAY 17Make Room for Joy+

Life brings responsibilities, bills, challenges, and goals that require our attention. Those things matter.

Today’s Action
Make time for one thing that brings you joy.

DAY 18A Little Patience Goes a Long Way+

We appreciate patience when someone gives us time to learn, understands a mistake, or recognizes that we may be carrying an unseen burden. Offering that same patience can be harder, especially when we're tired or frustrated.

Today’s Action
Choose patience during one frustrating moment.

DAY 19You Still Have Something to Offer+

Our circumstances and responsibilities change over time. A role that once defined us may end, or an opportunity may take a different shape.

Today’s Action
Offer a skill, a kind word, or a helping hand.

DAY 20Gratitude Over Taking Things for Granted+

It's easy to grow accustomed to the people, kindnesses, and everyday comforts that support us. We move through our routines assuming they'll always be there, and sometimes forget to express appreciation.

Today’s Action
Thank someone or something you might take for granted.

DAY 21Don't Let Fear Make Every Decision+

Fear has a purpose. It can help us recognize danger, prepare carefully, and consider the consequences of our choices.

Today’s Action
Take one manageable step despite uncertainty.

DAY 22Appreciate the People Who Show Up+

Some of the most important people in our lives aren't the ones making grand gestures. They're the ones who call, listen, remember what matters, and show up when life gets difficult.

Today’s Action
Tell someone you appreciate their presence.

DAY 23It's Okay to Change Direction+

Not every plan works as expected, and not every goal stays right for us. Experience may teach us something we couldn't have known at the beginning.

Today’s Action
Give yourself permission to reconsider an old plan.

DAY 24Speak With Care+

Words can comfort, encourage, and help someone feel understood. They can also leave a wound, especially when we speak in frustration without considering the effect.

Today’s Action
Pause before responding and choose your words carefully.

DAY 25Find Meaning in the Ordinary+

It's easy to believe that a meaningful day must include a major achievement or memorable event. Yet most of our lives unfold in ordinary places: at home, at work, and in the conversations between responsibilities.

Today’s Action
Notice one meaningful detail in an ordinary moment.

DAY 26Celebrate Someone Else+

We all appreciate recognition for the effort we put into our lives. But we also have the opportunity to recognize someone else's progress—a new skill, a personal milestone, or simply the courage to keep trying.

Today’s Action
Celebrate someone else's good news sincerely.

DAY 27Rest Is Part of the Journey+

When there's always more to do, rest can begin to feel like falling behind. We tell ourselves we'll slow down after the next responsibility, the next goal, or the next busy week.

Today’s Action
Give yourself time to rest without guilt.

DAY 28Let Your Actions Reflect Your Values+

It's easy to speak about kindness, honesty, patience, and respect when life is going smoothly. The challenge comes when we're tired, disappointed, or frustrated—and when no one is watching.

Today’s Action
Choose one action that reflects a value you cherish.

DAY 29Hope Can Begin Small+

Hope can be difficult when we don't know how a problem will end or how long a hard season will last. It doesn't have to mean pretending everything will work out exactly as we wish.

Today’s Action
Name one small reason to remain hopeful.

DAY 30The Difference You Make+

As we reach the end of these thirty days, I hope one thought stays with you: we rarely see the full effect of the good we do. A kind word may be remembered long after we've forgotten saying it.

Today’s Action
Pass along one kindness you once received.

Walk in peace. Live with purpose. Let your light shine.
